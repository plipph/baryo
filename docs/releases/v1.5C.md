# Version 1.5C — Business Growth

## Overview

Version 1.5C gives authorized businesses a structured way to publish time-bounded promotions and lets profiles claim them. It is planned, not implemented, and does not turn Listahan into a payment, POS, or delivery platform.

## Goals

- Help businesses promote local offers through existing storefront/discovery surfaces.
- Let authenticated profiles claim eligible offers safely.
- Provide measurable offer performance for business owners.
- Support controlled featured promotions without misleading users.

## Scope

- Business-created offers with lifecycle and visibility rules.
- Claimable offers and controlled coupon-code handling.
- Offer analytics for views, claims, and redemption-related events as defined in implementation.
- Featured promotion rules, placement, disclosure, and authorization.
- RLS, anti-abuse controls, expiry behavior, and documentation.

## Database Changes

Planned data includes `offers`, `offer_claims`, coupon/redemption attributes as approved, and offer analytics events. Define business/profile references, lifecycle states, expiry, limits, uniqueness, indexes, auditability, and RLS in migrations. The current `business_analytics`, `link_clicks`, and `item_clicks` tables are not substitutes for an offer lifecycle model.

## UI Changes

- Owner offer-management surfaces in the business workspace.
- Offer presentation on eligible public business/discovery surfaces.
- Authenticated claim and coupon display states.
- Owner offer analytics and featured-promotion disclosure.

## Routes Affected

Existing `/dashboard/*`, `/discover`, and `/{slug}` may receive offer flows. New dedicated offer routes/endpoints require approved route design. Existing routes are not renamed.

## Components Affected

Business workspace managers, public storefront/discovery cards, profile claim views, analytics views, and server-side offer authorization helpers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Only authorized business members can manage their business offers.
- Only authenticated eligible profiles can claim offers.
- Claims cannot exceed documented limits or bypass expiry/visibility rules.
- Coupon codes and redemption state are not exposed beyond the approved audience.
- Analytics distinguishes offer activity from existing business/link/item events.
- Featured placement is explicitly labeled and policy-controlled.
- Migrations, RLS, lint, build, and lifecycle/authorization tests pass.

## Out of Scope

Payments, checkout, food delivery, POS, inventory management, notifications/activity feed, and general advertising marketplace features.

## Risks

Offers create fraud, oversubscription, expiry, support, and business-staff workflow risks. Featured placements can erode trust without transparent policy. Analytics can be misleading if event definitions are not stable.

## Migration Strategy

Build on profile identity and future business membership authorization. Introduce the offer model with migrations and clear states, test eligibility and redemption paths against non-production data, release to a limited business cohort, then expand after monitoring.

## Future Compatibility

Offer claims feed Version 1.5D saved offers and notifications. Offer/claim records should remain compatible with future business staff roles, municipality promotions, and organization/government programs without encoding those roles prematurely.
