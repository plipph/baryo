# Version 1.5B — Community

## Overview

Version 1.5B adds the first consumer-facing community layer on top of the profile foundation. It is planned, not implemented. The release focuses on personal discovery signals and trustworthy feedback around existing public businesses.

## Goals

- Let signed-in profiles save businesses through favorites.
- Introduce moderated, attributable reviews.
- Define a public-profile experience appropriate to Listahan's local community.
- Establish a deliberate verified-reviewer policy.

## Scope

- Favorites for profile-to-business saving.
- Profile-authored reviews of businesses with moderation/publication state.
- Public profile presentation limited to approved, privacy-aware fields.
- Reviewer verification criteria and status presentation.
- Profile-based authorization, rate limiting, reporting/moderation operations, and documentation.

## Database Changes

Planned tables/fields include `favorites`, `reviews`, and profile attributes needed for public visibility and reviewer verification. Reviews require author, business, content/rating model, moderation state, timestamps, and uniqueness/abuse rules. Every change requires migrations, RLS, indexes, generated types, and a data-retention decision.

## UI Changes

- Favorite affordances on existing directory/business surfaces.
- Profile pages that can distinguish private account data from approved public identity.
- Review creation, display, empty, loading, moderation, and unavailable states.
- Clear verified-reviewer indicators that do not imply business verification.

## Routes Affected

Existing `/`, `/discover`, and `/{slug}` may gain community affordances. Profile/account routes introduced by Version 1.5A may gain public-profile views. No public route renaming is planned.

## Components Affected

Business cards, public storefront composition, route-local profile/review components, and shared permission/moderation helpers. Reuse existing visual patterns; do not refactor unrelated primitives.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A signed-in profile can save and remove its own favorites only.
- Review visibility follows an explicit moderation state.
- Reviewers can only manage their own permitted reviews.
- Public profiles expose only fields deliberately approved for public display.
- Verified-reviewer presentation has documented eligibility and revocation behavior.
- Anonymous public storefront browsing remains available.
- Migration, RLS, lint, build, and moderation test coverage pass before release.

## Out of Scope

Offers/claims/coupons, notifications, following, activity feeds, business memberships, billing, and a general social feed.

## Risks

Reviews create privacy, harassment, spam, and moderation burdens. Favorites affect performance on high-traffic listing pages. Public profiles need safe defaults and clear deletion/retention rules.

## Migration Strategy

Build on Version 1.5A profiles. Add normalized tables and policies in versioned migrations, backfill nothing unless required, release moderation tooling before broad review visibility, and monitor abuse/latency after rollout.

## Future Compatibility

Favorites support saved offers in Version 1.5D. Reviews and public profiles provide trust signals for future business verification, organization participation, and municipality-aware discovery.
